\textbf{Key Findings:}
(1) CB-HOD v1.1 reduces false positive rate from 100\% to 14.3\% (85.7pp reduction)
through pointer-tracking-based binding + interval constraint solving.
(2) PSVR-HLM v3.0 achieves 0\% FPR by layering source-level safety pattern recognition
(identical\_expr, loop\_guard\_break, ternary\_guard, etc.) atop CB-HOD's constraint engine.
(3) All three methods share one FN (CVE-2024-004 off-by-one heap write), representing
a fundamental limit of regex-based size expression extraction.
(4) Analysis time remains in the millisecond range — 3--4 orders of magnitude
faster than symbolic execution (Angr: 45s) or decompilation (Ghidra: 30s).
